SkarbIQ

Review guide

SkarbIQ reads Belarusian fiscal receipts by their QR code and sorts every purchased item into spending categories. The app UI is in Russian. No account is needed.

Receipt loading works only from Belarus. Receipts are loaded from ch.info-center.by, the public receipt verification service of the Belarusian tax authority, and from the e-receipt services of Belarusian retail chains. These services accept connections from Belarusian networks only. Outside Belarus, scanning a receipt QR code shows a message explaining this — that is expected behavior, not a bug. To review every feature, please load the built-in demo data as described below.

1. Load demo data

  1. Open the Настройки (Settings) tab — the last tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap Данные (Data).
  3. Tap Загрузить демо-данные (Load demo data), then Загрузить (Load).

About 190 receipts from the last six months are added, plus three recurring payments and a monthly income. Dates are shifted so the latest receipt is from yesterday. The same button also appears on the error screen after a failed scan: Посмотреть на демо-данных (View with demo data).

2. What to look at

Главная · Home

Spending for the week or month, savings from promotions, monthly income progress, spending by category, latest receipts.

Аналитика · Analytics

  • Spending by category and subcategory for a week, month, quarter or year. Tap a category for details.
  • Изменения цен (Price changes): how much the user’s basket became more expensive compared with the previous period, item by item.

История · History

  • Tap a receipt to expand its items; swipe left to edit or delete. The search field finds receipts by store or product name.
  • Open a receipt and tap the chart icon next to an item (for example, a cookie or milk item) — История цены (Price history): price chart, minimum and maximum, the store where it is cheaper now, all purchases.

Настройки · Settings

  • Регулярные платежи (Recurring payments): reminders and automatic recording of bills.
  • Доход в месяц (Monthly income), Лимиты категорий (Category limits) with notifications at 80% and 100%.
  • Данные → Экспортировать (Export to CSV), backups.

3. In-app purchases

Open Настройки → SkarbIQ Pro. Three options are offered:

  • Monthly auto-renewable subscription,
  • Yearly auto-renewable subscription with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers,
  • Lifetime — a one-time non-consumable purchase.

Pro unlocks price analytics (price history, price changes, price hints on new receipts), monthly income and unlimited recurring payments, CSV export and an unlimited receipt queue. Subscription terms, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links are shown on the same screen; Восстановить покупки restores purchases.

Everything else — scanning, categories, spending analytics, limits, widget and backups — is free.

4. Without receipts

Tap Добавить расход (Add expense) on Home → Добавить вручную (Add manually), enter an amount and a category, save. Works fully offline.

Good to know

  • Paper receipt recognition is temporarily disabled while it is being improved; its button is shown as inactive.
  • Улучшение категорий (Improving categories) in Settings is off by default. When enabled, it anonymously sends item names and their categories — no prices, dates or identifiers.
  • All receipts and purchases are stored only on the device. There is no account and no server of ours. Privacy policy
