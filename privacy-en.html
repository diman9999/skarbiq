Privacy Policy

In short: your receipts and purchases stay on your iPhone. Below is exactly what the app does with data and which services it contacts.

In short. Receipts, purchases, categories and settings are stored only on your device. There is no sign-up, no behavioural analytics and no advertising. If you choose to turn on “Help improve categories”, the app will anonymously send shop and item names together with their categories — without prices, dates, receipt codes or anything that points to you. On its own, the app only downloads shared files from the SkarbIQ website — the category dictionary and in-app news — without sending anything about you. Everything else happens only when you act: to load a receipt you have scanned or to subscribe through the App Store.

01Who processes the data

SkarbIQ is developed by an individual, Dzmitry Kandratovich. You can get in touch at kondor.dmitry@gmail.com.

02What the app creates

As you use the app, the following is saved on your device:

  • Receipts and purchases — shop, date, total, the list of items with prices, and receipt codes.
  • Categories and subcategories — both the built-in ones and any you create, together with the limits you set.
  • Your own corrections — when you change an item's category, the app remembers it so that it can classify the same item on its own next time.
  • Settings — your name if you entered one, notification preferences, and a counter of daily requests.

All of this is kept in a local database on your iPhone and in storage shared between the app and its widget. None of it is shared with the developer or with third parties. The only exception is described under “Helping improve categories” and applies only with your consent.

03Camera and photo access

The camera is used to read the QR code printed on a cash receipt. Photo access is used to read a QR code from an image you choose yourself.

Images are processed on the device itself, using the operating system's own facilities. The app does not store receipt photographs in its database and does not transmit them anywhere.

04Requests to the receipt verification service

To retrieve the contents of a purchase by its receipt code, the app opens the website ch.info-center.by in an embedded browser window. A request is only made when you act — after you have scanned a receipt or entered its code.

The site may ask you to sign in to an account — for example, once the number of checks allowed without signing in has been used up. You sign in personally, on the site's genuine page: the app does not ask for, see or store your password. Session data (cookies) is kept by the system's browser storage on your device.

When the site is contacted, it learns your IP address and the information a browser would normally provide. How the site handles that data is governed by its owner's policy, not by this one.

SkarbIQ is not affiliated with ch.info-center.by. The app is not an official client of that service and has no connection to its owner. It uses the publicly available receipt verification form in the same way a person would in a browser.

05Electronic receipts from retail chains

If a paper receipt carries only a QR code linking to a chain's electronic receipt (Dobronom, Kopeechka, Mayak — idcheck.dobronom.by; Evroopt — r.eplus.by), the app — when you scan it — requests that receipt from the chain's service, just as a browser would when opening the link. Only the receipt identifier from the QR code is sent. No sign-in is required. The link itself is not stored in the app — only the loaded receipt is, just as with a regular scan.

For receipts from iKassa cloud tills (links to receipts.cloud.ikassa.by), the app opens the receipt page from that link and reads the purchase from it. If that fails, only the date is taken from the page and the receipt is loaded in the usual way via ch.info-center.by.

For receipts from Webkassa tills (links to webkassa.by — for example, the Santa chain), the app downloads the receipt file from that link and reads the purchase from it. Only the receipt identifier from the QR code is sent.

The chain's or till provider's service receives your IP address and the information a browser would normally provide. Its handling of that data is governed by the service owner's policy (for example, Dobronom CJSC, Eurotorg LLC). SkarbIQ is not affiliated with these chains and services.

06Dictionary and news updates

On launch, the app checks the SkarbIQ website (diman9999.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages) and, if a newer version is available, downloads the category dictionary and the “What's new” text. These are the same public files for everyone: the app sends nothing — no receipts, purchases or identifiers. As with any website, the host receives the device's IP address; this is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

07SkarbIQ Pro subscription and purchases

SkarbIQ Pro subscriptions and the one-time purchase are made through the App Store. Payment is processed by Apple: the app does not receive or store your card details, name or Apple ID email address. From the App Store, the app only learns which plan is active and until when — in order to unlock Pro features. This information stays on the device and is not shared with the developer.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your iPhone settings (your name → Subscriptions) or in the app: Settings → SkarbIQ Pro → Manage subscription. Payment processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

08Protection against automated requests

The receipt verification page uses Google reCAPTCHA. When it loads, your browser contacts Google's servers, which may receive your IP address and technical details about your device. The terms are described in Google's privacy policy.

09Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app will alert you when a limit you have set is being approached and, if you wish, remind you to record receipts. Notifications are generated on the device. The app does not use push notifications.

10Export and backups

You can export your data to a CSV file or create a backup in JSON format. The file is created on your command, and you decide where to send or store it. The app does not upload these files anywhere automatically.

11Helping improve categories

This feature stays off until you turn it on yourself — in the settings or in reply to a one-time question in the app. You can turn it off at any time: Settings → “Improving categories”.

While the feature is on, the app sends the developer records made up of the following fields:

  • the shop name as printed on the receipt;
  • the item name as stored in the app, and its barcode if the till provided one;
  • the item's category and who chose it — the app or you;
  • the app version.

Never sent: prices, totals and discounts, purchase dates and times, receipt codes and electronic receipt links, your name, notes, manually entered expenses, or any device or account identifiers. When you turn the feature on, items from receipts saved earlier are sent as well.

Records are collected on the device and sent no more than once an hour — shuffled and without duplicates: the same item is sent only once. As a result, they cannot be used to reconstruct an individual purchase or to link records to a particular person.

The sole purpose is to help the app recognise item categories and shop names more accurately. Aggregated records may become part of the app's built-in category dictionary and ship in app updates.

Records are received by Google Apps Script and stored in a Google spreadsheet owned by the developer. As with any request over the internet, Google receives the device's IP address; the script does not see or store it. The date records were received is kept to the day. Processing on Google's side is governed by Google's privacy policy. Records are kept for as long as they are needed to improve the category dictionary.

The legal basis for processing is your consent. If you withdraw it, records not yet sent are deleted from the device immediately. Records already sent cannot be deleted on request: they contain nothing that would distinguish them from other users' records.

12Analytics and advertising

The app contains no advertising, no ad networks and no behavioural analytics. It does not track you across other apps or websites and does not request permission to do so.

13Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you want it to. You can remove it in two ways:

  • in the app's settings — the "Clear all data" option;
  • by deleting the app from your device, which removes its database along with it.

The developer holds no copies of your receipts, so deleted data cannot be recovered — unless you made a backup yourself beforehand. The “Clear all data” option also deletes any records not yet sent for improving categories.

14Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

15Your rights

Your receipts and purchases never leave your device, so you control them directly: you can view, edit, export and delete them at any time without contacting the developer. You can turn participation in improving categories on or off in the settings at any time. If you have questions, write to kondor.dmitry@gmail.com.

16Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
