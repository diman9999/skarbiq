Privacy Policy

SkarbIQ for iOS · Last updated 26.09.2026

In short. The app does not collect your data and does not send it to us. Receipts, items, categories and settings are stored only on your device. We run no servers that hold anything about you, there is no sign-up, and there is no behavioural analytics.

Who processes the data

SkarbIQ is developed by an individual, Dzmitry Kandratovich. You can get in touch at SUPPORT EMAIL.

What the app creates

As you use the app, the following is saved on your device:

  • Receipts and purchases — shop, date, total, the list of items with prices, and receipt codes.
  • Categories and subcategories — both the built-in ones and any you create, together with the limits you set.
  • Your own corrections — when you change an item's category, the app remembers it so that it can classify the same item on its own next time.
  • Settings — your name if you entered one, notification preferences, and a counter of daily requests.

All of this is kept in a local database on your iPhone and in storage shared between the app and its widget. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Camera and photo access

The camera is used to read the QR code printed on a cash receipt and to photograph a paper receipt. Photo access is used to read a QR code from an image you choose yourself.

Images are processed on the device itself, using the operating system's own facilities. The app does not store receipt photographs in its database and does not transmit them anywhere.

Requests to the receipt verification service

To retrieve the contents of a purchase by its receipt code, the app opens the website ch.info-center.by in an embedded browser window. A request is only made when you act — after you have scanned a receipt and specified its date.

That service requires you to sign in to an account on the site itself. You sign in personally, on the site's genuine page: the app does not ask for, see or store your password. Session data (cookies) is kept by the system's browser storage on your device.

When the site is contacted, it learns your IP address and the information a browser would normally provide. How the site handles that data is governed by its owner's policy, not by this one.

SkarbIQ is not affiliated with ch.info-center.by. The app is not an official client of that service and has no connection to its owner. It uses the publicly available receipt verification form in the same way a person would in a browser.

Protection against automated requests

The receipt verification page uses Google reCAPTCHA. When it loads, your browser contacts Google's servers, which may receive your IP address and technical details about your device. The terms are described in Google's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app will alert you when a limit you have set is being approached and, if you wish, remind you to record receipts. Notifications are generated on the device. There are no push notifications from our servers — there are no servers either.

Export and backups

You can export your data to a CSV file or create a backup in JSON format. The file is created on your command, and you decide where to send or store it. The app does not upload these files anywhere automatically.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no advertising, no ad networks and no behavioural analytics. It does not track you across other apps or websites and does not request permission to do so.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you want it to. You can remove it in two ways:

  • in the app's settings — the "Clear all data" option;
  • by deleting the app from your device, which removes its database along with it.

Because the developer holds no copies, deleted data cannot be recovered — unless you made a backup yourself beforehand.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Your rights

Since the data never leaves your device, you control it directly: you can view, edit, export and delete it at any time without contacting the developer. If you have questions, write to SUPPORT EMAIL.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page with a new date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
